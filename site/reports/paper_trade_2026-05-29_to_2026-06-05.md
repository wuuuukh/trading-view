## Weekly Stock Scan

Every Sunday, refresh the candidate pool using the user's selection logic:

- Use only data from 2026 onward unless the user explicitly asks for long historical context
- Prefer daily/weekly/monthly structure alignment
- Check weekly price-up-volume-up behavior
- Check whether the stock meets the shareholder/chip proxy condition from the existing weekly selection process
- Separate "structure qualified" from "operation candidate"
- Prefer cleaner line patterns, reasonable volume, and confluence zones
- Avoid forcing user-mentioned tickers into operation status

The Sunday scan can add, downgrade, or remove watchlist names. A newly scanned stock can be operated during the same target week. It is not automatically bought from the scan alone: it must still pass market condition, daily 3MA/8MA checks, 5K entry confirmation, 60K MACD confirmation, and the existing defense rules during market hours. Exception: when price pulls back to monthly K 3MA, the updated rule allows direct buying without waiting for 5K to turn red/strong.

Intraday classification rule:

- Weekly updates still use the original static buckets: operation candidate, observation candidate, defense/elimination, holding observation, and unheld observation.
- During market hours, every tracked stock is watched at the same time, including observation candidates.
- Observation candidates are not checked only after the main operation group has no opportunity.
- If an observation candidate stands back above daily 3MA, holds above daily 8MA with a valid reversal, or pulls back into a weekly/monthly 3MA confluence zone, it is dynamically upgraded into the operation group for that session. If the pullback reaches monthly K 3MA, buy directly without waiting for 5K to turn red.
- The dynamic upgrade is for intraday action only; the weekly report still records the stock under the original weekly classification until the next scan refreshes it.

## Entry Logic

Entry is allowed only when the stock already passed the daily/weekly/monthly structure screen and then shows intraday confirmation.

Required confirmation:

- Market is not in panic mode
- On the first trading day of each week and each month, calculate each locked stock's weekly 3MA and monthly 3MA immediately after the 09:00 open
- Weekly volume has satisfied price-up-volume-up logic, or daily structure is in attack mode
- Daily structure keeps the key attack line
- Monthly K 3MA pullback direct-buy rule: if price pulls back to monthly K 3MA, buy directly and do not wait for 5K to turn red
- 5K shows red-red-red, one-star-two-yang continuation, or volume contraction followed by a red K
- No consecutive volume-expanding black 5K candles
- 60K MACD is improving or not deteriorating

## Defense Logic

- Every market open, check all held stocks first. If a held stock opens below daily 3MA, immediately reduce 1/2 and move the remaining position to daily 8MA defense.
- If price breaks daily 3MA and cannot recover quickly, no new entry
- If a bought position later closes below daily 3MA and the opening defense did not already execute, reduce 1/2 on the next valid sell day
- If price breaks daily 8MA, exit the short-term trade
- If the weekly K remains healthy but daily K breaks, downgrade to observation instead of adding
